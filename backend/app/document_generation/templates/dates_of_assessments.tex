\assessment{Midterm and endterm tests}
These are two longer written tests taking place during the semester. They are aimed at measuring your knowledge and problem-solving skills based on the content of the practice classes. In order to prepare for them, you will need to practice and revise the problems solved in the practice classes. Also, you should aim to learn and understand the relevant theory covered in the lectures, because the problems solved in the classes are built on this theory. Each of the two tests can be successful or unsuccessful. See the separately configured passing scores and success criteria below.
\field{Midterm retake rules}{@@MIDTERM_RETAKE_RULES@@}
\field{Endterm retake rules}{@@ENDTERM_RETAKE_RULES@@}
\field{Sample papers}{Closer to the time of each test, a sample paper will be shared in Canvas, showing the format, structure, approximate level of difficulty, and the broader topics covered by the real test. Note that the sample test contains just one possible selection of questions from the syllabus. The real test will be somewhat similar to this, but it will not necessarily contain the same types of questions.}
