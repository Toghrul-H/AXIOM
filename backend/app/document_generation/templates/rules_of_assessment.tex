Computer Science BSc, Discrete Mathematics I practice\\
Rules of assessment for the practice course (@@SEMESTER@@)

The Discrete Mathematics I subject is delivered in two courses: the lecture
course and the practice course. Accordingly, you will obtain two grades in the
subject by the end of the semester: a practice grade (associated with the practice
classes) and a theory grade (associated with the lectures). This document explains
the rules of establishing your practice grade.

In the practice classes we are going to solve questions in the following topics:

Canvas in due course. These are the questions which definitely need to be solved,
in class or as homework, and the assignments will be based on questions similar to these.

\subsection*{Midterm and endterm tests}
They are aimed at measuring your knowledge and problem-solving skills based on the
content of the practice classes. In order to prepare for them, you will need to
practise and revise the problems solved in the practice classes. Also, you should
aim to learn and understand the relevant theory covered in the lectures, because
the problems solved in the classes are built on this theory.

\subsection*{Absence from the midterm or the endterm test}
In rare cases, you may have some serious reason beyond your control for being
absent from a test. In such cases, you need to notify and provide written evidence
for the reason of your absence to your practice class teacher before the
assessment, or if it is impossible, as soon as feasibly possible.
Please follow the attendance and course-passing rules stated above.

\subsection*{Replacement test}
Replacement eligibility and any exceptional arrangements follow the attendance,
course-passing and retake rules stated above. The scheduled midterm/endterm retake
is @@TEST_RETAKE_DATE@@, @@TEST_RETAKE_TIME@@. Any separately authorised arrangement
will be confirmed by your practice class teacher.
