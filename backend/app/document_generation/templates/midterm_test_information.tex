\heading{At the beginning}
@@MIDTERM_ARRIVAL_SENTENCE@@
After arrival, please take a seat according to the seating plan and prepare the
permitted equipment. Please follow the instructions of the invigilators.

\heading{During the test}
Please do not use any forbidden equipment and do not talk or communicate with each
other. If you need help, please indicate this to one of the invigilators. Note that
the invigilators cannot help with solving the questions or checking your solutions
or give you feedback or answer mathematical questions during the test.

\heading{At the end of the test}
If you would like to take a photo of your paper after finishing and before
submitting it, please notify an invigilator about this; do not take your mobile
phone/camera out of your bag without permission.

\heading{After the test}
The midterm test paper will be uploaded to and made available on Canvas soon after
the test. Your practice class teacher will offer your practice group a viewing
time, when you can look at the marking of your paper and obtain feedback on it.

\textbf{Thank you and all the best!}

The midterm test is an assignment associated with the practice classes. Your result
in this test counts towards your practice grade. For detailed information about the
practice grade and the rules of assessment in the practice classes, please see the
document titled ``Rules of attendance and assessment in the practice classes''
available on the Canvas page of your practice class.
